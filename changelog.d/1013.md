- Crawls now report four missing security headers per HTML page from the stored final response: `MISSING_CSP`, `MISSING_X_CONTENT_TYPE_OPTIONS`, `MISSING_X_FRAME_OPTIONS` (satisfied by a CSP `frame-ancestors` directive) and `MISSING_REFERRER_POLICY`. Pages whose stored headers cannot be parsed are counted as unmeasured, not clean, and a crawl without a stored scan skips them with a reason.
