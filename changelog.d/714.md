- `metrika-report` (`seo_metrika_report`) and `MetrikaClient.report()`: a Reporting API
  "Query is too complicated" refusal is now retried in calendar-month slices with a 5–15 s
  backoff; a slice that still refuses is resubmitted at a sampled `accuracy=0.1`. Rows are
  merged across the slices (metric sums per dimension key, the caller's `sort` re-applied,
  the `limit`/`offset` window taken last), and the merged body states what happened:
  `split` lists the slices and the accuracy each needed, `accuracy_used` gives the least
  precise one, and `sampled`/`sample_share` reflect the merged answer. Sliced sums keep
  additive metrics exact; distinct-visitor and ratio metrics are approximations, which
  `split.note` says explicitly. A period that cannot be mapped to dates (a shorthand such
  as `month`) is still retried and sampled before the error is re-raised. An explicit
  `accuracy` in the request params is now honored instead of being overridden to `full`.
