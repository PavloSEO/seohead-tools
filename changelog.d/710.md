- `duplicate-check --scan` and `boilerplate-report --scan` now stream a retained
  corpus instead of dying on normal-size sites: the scan's open-and-validate
  deadline is budgeted by artifact size, and each document read gets its own
  deadline, so a 1.5k-page corpus no longer ends in `interrupted`. The corpus
  input-byte budget meters what the analyzer actually retains — extracted
  Markdown for duplicates, one digest per page for boilerplate — not raw HTML
  size, and reaching a corpus bound reports partial coverage with the exact
  prepared/eligible counts and the bound that stopped the read (#710).
