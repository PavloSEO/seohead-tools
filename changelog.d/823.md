- Add objective authorship, date and trust-page evidence to scoped audits (issue #823),
  eight notice-severity checks in `seohead/sf/core/eeat.py` that report what a crawl can
  observe and never an E-E-A-T score. `NO_AUTHOR_BYLINE` and `NO_CONTENT_DATES` fire only on
  content-shaped pages -- a JSON-LD article type, `og:type=article`, an `<article>` element,
  or a conventional content path -- and name the carriers they read (`meta_author`,
  `rel_author`, `itemprop=author`, `byline` class, `<time datetime>`, JSON-LD
  `author`/`datePublished`/`dateModified`, a date-shaped URL path), each with a declared
  confidence. `MISSING_ABOUT_PAGE`, `MISSING_CONTACT_PAGE`, `MISSING_PRIVACY_POLICY` and
  `MISSING_TERMS_PAGE` report one of `found_indexable` (silent), `found_non_indexable`,
  `found_error`, `discovered_not_crawled` or `not_discovered`, so a page that exists but does
  not serve never reads the same as one nobody linked. `FEW_CITATIONS` reports only what
  link positions can prove -- an outbound link inside the body copy, or a declared count of
  zero -- and `YMYL_REVIEW_CANDIDATE` flags deterministic whole-word path/title matches for
  human review, never a classification. Unmeasured stays distinct from absent throughout:
  a page whose body was never parsed keeps `trust_signals` NULL and is counted in the skip
  reason, and `summary.trust_evidence` records the scope evaluated, the per-kind page state,
  and a partial-crawl caveat for `not_discovered` verdicts.
