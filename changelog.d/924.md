- `scan-link-inspect --view links` (MCP `seo_scan_link_inspect`) pages the outgoing or incoming hyperlinks of a URL, looked up by address rather than document ID: anchor, `rel` with nofollow/sponsored/ugc flags, internal/external, stored position, `target` attribute and the HTTP status of the other end (`null` when that page was not scanned). Filters by link type, follow, status class and substring; `offset`/`limit` up to 200; `total`/`filtered_total` are exact or flagged capped. The scan is opened read-only without full validation, and failures carry a machine `reason_code` (`url_not_found`, `scan_not_available`, `url_not_scanned`, `links_not_retained`). Existing views and fields are unchanged.
