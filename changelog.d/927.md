- Added `scan-url-query` (MCP `seo_scan_url_query`): a read-only, server-side filtered, sorted and paginated read of the whole page table of one saved scan, with `total`, `filtered_total` (`null` plus `filtered_total_state: capped` when the count exceeds its time budget), a column projection, scan coverage labels, the `seohead.scan-url-query.v1` format and a machine-readable `reason_code` on every failure. At most 200 rows per call; sorting by `url`, `status_code`, `page_ordinal` or `url_id` uses indexes, and any other column needs a filter that leaves at most 100,000 rows (otherwise `sort_not_indexed`). The scan is opened with a light identity and schema-generation check, not full validation, and is never modified.
