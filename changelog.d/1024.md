- `crawl-enrich` reports four web-analytics findings from an offline URL-keyed CSV: analytics orphan URLs, indexable pages with no analytics data, non-indexable pages with visits, and bounce rate above 70%. Named columns `--visits-column` and `--bounce-column` select the data; a missing column is refused by name, unreadable values are counted and never read as zero, and analytics orphans are withheld on a partial crawl. Live per-URL Metrika/GA4 sourcing is not included yet (#990, #984).
- The SF issue coverage map lists the four web-analytics issues (Orphan URLs, Bounce Rate Above 70%, No GA Data, Non-Indexable with GA Data) as found by `crawl-enrich` from an offline CSV instead of out of scope. `sf run` still does not emit them.
