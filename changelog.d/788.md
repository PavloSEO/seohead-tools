- Added the `ledger.v1` remediation ledger (`seohead.storage.ledger`, `SEOL` application id): a separate project-bound SQLite artifact that records findings, per-occurrence identity, affected-URL membership and append-only observation history from validated `scan.v1`/`scan.v2` audits without mutating the source scans. Ingestion binds each source by scan UUID plus evidence revision, audit digest and producer/configuration identity; exact replays are idempotent, conflicting bytes under the same revision are refused atomically, and `capped`/`aggregate_only` coverage plus `unavailable` evidence are retained as data rather than silently treated as resolved. Includes transactional 0→1 migration, full schema/integrity validation on open, and `docs/LEDGER.md` (#788).
